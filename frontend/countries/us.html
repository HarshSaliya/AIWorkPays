AI work platforms in United States

The United States has the fewest eligibility restrictions of any country here — nearly every platform on the ledger accepts US applicants, and domestic payout rails clear quickly.

What to check first

  • Most platforms treat US contributors as independent contractors, which carries its own tax filing obligations. Check the platform's stated classification before starting.
  • Direct bank transfer and PayPal are both common, so withdrawal fees are rarely the deciding factor.
  • Because eligibility is rarely the constraint, the entry gate is what to optimise for — the quiz sorts on exactly that.

Compiled from each platform's published eligibility and payout terms, last checked Sep 2026. Not written from personal experience of working from the United States. Eligibility changes without notice — confirm on the platform before investing time.
