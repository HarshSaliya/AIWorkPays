About

What this is

AI Work Pays is a ledger of platforms that pay people to train and evaluate AI models. It exists because the alternative — scraped listicles with no dates, no eligibility information and undisclosed affiliate links — wastes people's time.

The rules this site runs on

  • Every pay figure is labelled as advertised, with the month it was checked.
  • Every outbound link states whether it is a referral link before you click it.
  • Every listing that carries a catch says so, in the same place as the pay figure.
  • Nothing is claimed as personal experience unless it is personal experience.

How it is maintained

Listings are reviewed against each platform's published terms and public contributor reports, and the last checked date on every row is the date that review happened. When a rate or an eligibility rule changes, the row changes.

Who runs it

Your words go here

One short paragraph: your name, what you do, and why you started this. Keep it to three or four sentences — in this category a short honest bio outperforms a long one.

Then a contact address, so corrections have somewhere to go.

How it makes money

Referral bonuses from some of the platforms listed, disclosed on every affected link. The full policy is on the referrals and disclosure page.
