Referrals & disclosure

Some links here are referral links. If you sign up through one, the platform pays a small bonus back to this site. It never changes what you're paid, and it never changes your terms — the platform decides your acceptance on your assessment or interview, exactly as it would otherwise. Why the money side barely matters here is on the about page.

How you can tell

  • A listing with a referral link shows a filled green button and the line "Referral link — I earn a bonus if you sign up through it."
  • A listing without one shows an outlined button and the line "Direct link — no referral relationship, so this listing earns nothing."

Every outbound link on this site carries rel="nofollow", and referral links additionally carry rel="sponsored".

Every platform, and whether I hold a link

The whole list, in one place. Where a referral link exists you can see what you get through it and what has to happen before it counts. Where none exists, it says so and the listing earns nothing.

What this table deliberately does not publish is what I earn per signup — only that a referral relationship exists, which is the part that matters to you. Terms are set by the platforms and change without notice.

What the status tag means

Paying means the platform pays its contributors and is operating, judged from its own published terms and from public worker reports, on the month shown in the last checked field.

It is not a claim that I personally earned money there, and it is not a promise you will be accepted. Where a platform's terms carry a catch — a withdrawal minimum, a maturity hold, an unpaid qualification exam — it is written into the Honest note on that listing rather than left out.

Pay figures

Every rate on this site is what the platform advertises, tagged with the month it was checked. Advertised rates are frequently the top of a band that few contributors reach. Treat them as a ceiling, not an expectation.

Ordering

The default order of the ledger is not sold, and no platform has paid for placement. Quiz results are ranked by a scoring function against your answers — you can sort the ledger yourself by advertised pay or alphabetically at any time.

Corrections

If a rate, an eligibility rule or a payout term on this site is out of date or wrong, tell me and I will change it. Listings are re-checked periodically and each one carries the date it was last looked at.
