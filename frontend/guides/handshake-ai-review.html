My Handshake AI review

Mixed, leaning negative. The work itself was fine — the review process is what made it exhausting.

4/10

My rating

Worth trying if you're curious, but go in knowing the approval process is frustrating and the pay-to-effort ratio is poor.

My experience with Handshake's AI tasking work was mixed, leaning negative. I worked on one of their AI tasking projects, and the problems were almost entirely on the review side, not the work itself.

The review process is the biggest issue

On one project I submitted a task that met every stated requirement, got it approved, and then had it rejected seven separate times afterward — often for reasons that contradicted the earlier approval, or for automated checker flags that were clearly misfiring (quoting the wrong field, claiming "no question" when the question was right there). I spent days re-submitting the same correct work. For the effort required, the pay does not match up.

Too many people, not enough work

It also feels like they onboard far more people onto a project than the work actually needs, so you spend a lot of unpaid time fighting the system and competing for limited tasks rather than getting paid for output.

What actually worked

On the positive side: the work itself is genuinely interesting if you like AI/ML and problem design, the task guidelines are detailed, and the reviewers in the Slack channel do respond when you reach out directly. The concept has potential.

Overall

Worth trying if you're curious about AI evaluation work, but go in knowing the review/approval process can be frustrating and the pay-to-effort ratio is poor.

This is one person's direct experience on one project and may not reflect what you'd see — projects, reviewers and task quality vary. Rated independently of any referral relationship below.
