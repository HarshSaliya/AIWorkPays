Which platforms have no interview at all?

If you don't want a video call or a long assessment, this is the short list — and what each one costs you in pay.

What "no interview" costs you

There is a fairly consistent trade-off: the platforms with no gate tend to sit lower in the rate band. That is not a coincidence — the assessment is how platforms justify paying more for higher-quality output, so removing it tends to remove the premium too.

The exception is worth knowing about. A small number of platforms offer genuinely mid-band rates with no interview, usually because they're recruiting at volume for research projects. Those are the ones to apply to first.

What replaces the interview

Rarely nothing at all. Typically it's a lightweight check appropriate to the work — a microphone test for voice platforms, a short sample recording, or a brief training module you have to pass before better-paid task queues unlock. Faster and lower-stakes than an assessment, but not zero.

A realistic plan

  1. Start with a no-gate platform to get money moving this week.
  2. In parallel, take one written assessment at a higher-rate platform.
  3. Move your hours across when the second one comes through.
