My AfterQuery review

Disappointing as a candidate, mainly down to poor communication and a lack of transparency.

My experience with AfterQuery as a candidate was disappointing, mainly due to poor communication and a lack of transparency.

Assigned a project, then silence

I was assigned to a project and told it would start "from tomorrow." Then there was no response for three days. I followed up and was again told it would start the next day — and again, nothing for several more days. Finally, I was simply told the project was cancelled. At no point did anyone proactively keep me informed; I had to chase every update myself.

Three recurring problems

  • Too few real projects for the number of people they onboard, so you're left waiting indefinitely.
  • No proper or timely responses — updates are vague ("starts tomorrow") and then go silent.
  • No transparency — you complete assessments, verification and profile setup, then get left in the dark about whether a project is actually coming.

Support didn't help either

Reaching out produced the same copy-paste explanation of their selection process instead of addressing the actual issue, and escalating to a human agent came with a multi-day wait.

To be fair

The onboarding is organized, the work itself can be interesting, and the concept has real potential. But the candidate experience needs serious work. Assigning a project and then cancelling it with no communication wastes people's time and shows little respect for the effort candidates put in.

Overall

Only worth it if you have patience to spare and treat it as a long shot, not a reliable source of work.

This is one person's direct experience as a candidate and may not reflect what you'd see — timing and project availability vary. Rated independently of any referral relationship below.
